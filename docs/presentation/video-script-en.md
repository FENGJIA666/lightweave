## Capture and edit notes

- Record the start-to-three-blooms-to-reset sequence as one continuous master take. Derive close-ups from that take or clearly matched additional Preview captures.
- Use the real Preview environment as the backdrop. Golden light, colorful buds, clear silhouettes, and restrained labels carry the composition.
- Keep input and resulting beam movement visible together when explaining interaction.
- Let the first bloom and final completion breathe; use the pauses for visual understanding.
- Use natural English narration at a measured pace, with room for actions and short pauses. Final timing is set by the captured interaction and spoken read.
- Keep the exported edit at 150 seconds or less, leaving comfortable room below the official three-minute limit.
- Export public captions in English. Keep the Chinese review copy as the approval reference.
